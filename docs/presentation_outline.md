# Presentation template and timing

Guide pp.5,7-8: PDF/PPTX, **at most 12 slides**; nine mandatory content topics; team slot 5-minute presentation, 4-minute questions, 1-minute transition. This ten-slide plan and timing are team proposals. Source every business fact; visibly label estimates. No challenge, product name, results or figures have been invented.

| Slide | Seconds | Content | Evidence to insert |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 Problem and user | 30 | Actual assigned problem, affected user, scale/pain statistic. | Direct source/date; one clear sentence. |
| 2 Solution and RAG architecture | 35 | Data -> ingestion/index -> retrieval -> grounded generation -> UI. Explain why RAG. | Actual model/config, local/hosted boundaries. |
| 3 Working demonstration | 50 | Real question, cited answer and source; brief OOS refusal. | Actual UI/video/screenshots; no fabricated outputs. |
| 4 Evaluation | 25 | 10+ reference questions, defined P@K/R@K/faithfulness, latency, worst failure and improvement. | Measured values only, sample sizes and limitations. |
| 5 Market | 25 | TAM/SAM/SOM and initial segment. | Sourced inputs, transparent assumptions/units. |
| 6 Revenue and pricing | 25 | Buyer, price, justification, CAC/LTV/margin. | Sources and explicitly estimated inputs. |
| 7 Costs and forecast | 25 | Operating cost, 12-36-month forecast, break-even. | Formula/assumptions; no-break-even if applicable. |
| 8 Marketing | 25 | Persona, channels, budget, KPIs, 90-day plan. | Targets distinguished from actuals. |
| 9 Alternatives and innovation | 25 | Current alternatives and one useful proven differentiator. | Comparison/ablation, constraints. |
| 10 Team, next steps and ask | 20 | Actual roles, post-event plan, final ask. | Honest ownership and specific next step. |
| Buffer | 15 | Transition or demo delay. | Stop at five minutes. |

Total planned speech/demo: 285 seconds plus 15 seconds buffer = 300 seconds. Do not add full appendix slides beyond the 12-slide cap. Keep detailed run reports/code/source ledger outside the deck and accessible for questions.

## Backup demonstration

The guide accepts recorded demonstration equally to live for the practical-evidence subcriterion. Optional submission video is 2-3 minutes; preferred contents are three real questions including a cited answer and an OOS refusal. The in-talk 50-second segment may be an excerpt; it is not the full optional submission recording.

Rehearse switching to an authentic recording if network fails. Clearly identify recorded output. Open the final exported deck, check Arabic fonts/RTL and verify that source footnotes/links are legible. Test external video links or package a local file as permitted by submission platform.
